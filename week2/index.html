Multi-State Accordion

Mode: Single-Open (Collapsing)
Independent encapsulated component instances manage their own DOM state without leaking global variables or colliding with surrounding page elements.
Use Up and Down arrow keys to cycle focus between accordion headers. Press Enter or Space to toggle panels.
