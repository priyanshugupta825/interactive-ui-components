Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started, billing, and system compatibility.

How does the 14-day free trial work? +

You receive full, unrestricted access to our Professional tier for 14 days without entering any credit card information. You can connect repositories, run automated pipelines, and test edge rollouts. When your trial ends, you can choose a paid tier or stay on our free developer tier.

Which cloud providers and orchestrators are supported? +

NovaCloud supports AWS (EKS, ECS, Lambda), Google Cloud (GKE, Cloud Run), Microsoft Azure (AKS, Container Apps), and bare-metal Kubernetes clusters. Our CLI and agent auto-discover your cluster configuration within minutes.

How does NovaCloud ensure our code and data remain secure? +

We adhere to strict zero-knowledge principles. Your source code and runtime environment variables are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit via TLS 1.3. We undergo annual SOC2 Type II and ISO 27001 third-party audits.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing at any time? +

Yes! You can switch plans or change your billing interval at any point directly from the workspace settings dashboard. Any unused prorated balance will be automatically applied as credit.

Do you offer custom onboarding and migration assistance? +

Yes! For Enterprise customers, our Solutions Engineering team provides white-glove architecture reviews, CI/CD pipeline migration scripts, and dedicated training sessions for your engineering staff.
